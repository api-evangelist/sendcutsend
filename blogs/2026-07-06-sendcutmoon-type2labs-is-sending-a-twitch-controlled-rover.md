Most people use Twitch Chat to connect with gamers and creators in real-time. Will Brennan, founder of Type2Labs, wants to use it to drive a rover on the moon. His mission, “Chat, We’re Going to the Moon,” is exactly as it sounds: a lunar rover that Twitch Chat can collectively control from Earth.
